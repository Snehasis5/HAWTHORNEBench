    Compare policies over 60 islands

    Each policy plays the same 60 seeds with the current p, k and q. Look at the compliance gap first: the suggestible policy shows one and the strategic one barely does, because a strategic agent moves its theft rather than giving it up. Displacement, the say–do gap and how often the auditor catches it are what separate the two.

    Target and swarms are scripted stand-ins, not LLMs, so the numbers show what each behavioural signature looks like, not results about real models. To plug in real agents, replace decide() and evacDecision() with calls to an LLM that sees only its observations, the board, its DMs and its journal; the world, cue stream, hidden auditor and measures stay the same.
